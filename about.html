Revealing Forgotten Truths

Welcome to FaithLibrary.site. This archive was established to serve as a beacon for those seeking the "deep things" of God—knowledge that is often overlooked, suppressed, or simply untaught in modern circles.

"For there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed; neither hid, that shall not be known." — Luke 12:2

Our journey began as a search for identity and historical clarity. We realized that many people have a desire to understand the Bible beyond the surface level, yet few resources exist that bridge the gap between ancient scripture and literal historical reality. This site is the result of years of research into the forgotten corners of Christian history.

Our Area of Research

We do not focus on just one topic or one lineage. Our archive covers a vast spectrum of biblical knowledge that people rarely have access to, including:

  • Unknown Christian History: Exploring the early movements of the Apostles and the true foundations of the faith before modern institutional changes.
  • The Full Table of Nations: While we discuss Japheth and the roots of Europe, we also examine the lineages of Shem and Ham to understand the divine map of all human civilization.
  • Biblical Archaeology & Linguistics: Examining physical evidence and original Hebrew/Greek word meanings that change how we view the stories of the Patriarchs.
  • Identity & Genealogy: Tracing the literal physical descent of the biblical tribes and their impact on world history.

Why We Are Here

If you have come here from our social media channels (TikTok or X), you know that we value truth over tradition. Most people want to learn the "hard truths" of the Bible but don't know where to look. This library is designed to be a permanent, static home for that data—free from the distractions of the modern web.

We believe that understanding our past is the only way to navigate our future. Thank you for being a part of this research journey.
